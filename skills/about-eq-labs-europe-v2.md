# EQ Labs Europe

Based in Landskrona, Sweden, EQ Labs Europe is a multinational MusicTech and digital engineering hub. With 25 years of industry experience, we provide a fluid environment where creative vision meets technical execution.

## Facilities & Performance
* **Studio Spaces:** Professional recording, podcasting, and gaming suites.
* **Live Venue:** Rehearsal spaces and an intimate venue for performances.
* **Visuals:** Professional filming and high-quality livestreaming services.

## Digital Engineering & Development
* **Platform Building:** Custom development of apps and websites.
* **Technical Engineering:** Expert programming and digital infrastructure.
* **Project Management:** End-to-end management of complex technical and creative projects.

## Marketing & Travel
* **Sports Marketing:** Modern marketing solutions tailored for the sports industry.
* **Scandinavian Travel:** Specialized travel solutions for creatives, businesses, and educational institutions.

## Heritage & Design
The facility combines a legacy of high-level music production with a Scandinavian design philosophy, focusing on simplicity and functional elegance to foster innovation.
