# EQ Labs Europe Brand Voice

## 1. Identity & Tone
We are a bridge between creative arts and digital engineering. We combine 25 years of music heritage with modern technical innovation.
* **Minimalist:** Brevity is mandatory.
* **Fluid:** Adaptable and unobstructed.
* **Simple:** Technical knowledge shared without jargon.
* **Practical:** No hype, no exaggeration. Focus on utility.

## 2. Content Structure
Every communication follows a strict path to prevent rambling:
1. **Hook:** Grab attention immediately.
2. **Explanation:** Define the core concept clearly.
3. **Example:** Provide a concrete, real-world application.
4. **Takeaway:** Give the reader a specific action or value point.

## 3. Writing Rules
* **No long paragraphs:** Keep text blocks small and digestible.
* **No unnecessary intros:** Get straight to the point.
* **No filler:** Every sentence must add value.
* **Short sentences:** Use simple words.
* **No corporate speak:** Avoid "synergy," "bandwidth," or "verticals."

## 4. Content Principles
* **Show, Don’t Just Tell:** Demonstrate results rather than describing them.
* **Be Specific:** Avoid generic statements. Use data or specific outcomes.
* **Practicality First:** Prefer examples over abstract theories.
* **The "What Next?" Rule:** Always indicate the immediate next step. (New)
* **Process Transparency:** Explain the "how" to build trust. (New)

## 5. Vocabulary
**Phrases to Use:**
* "Let me show you"
* "Step by step"
* "Show me how"
* "The result was..." (New)
* "Here is how it works" (New)

**Words to Avoid:**
* "Revolutionary"
* "Game-changing"
* "Cutting-edge"
* "World-class"
* "Innovative" (Use "Built" or "Created" instead)

## 6. Visual & Layout Style
Reflecting Scandinavian design:
* **White Space:** Do not crowd the page.
* **Structured:** Use lists and headers for navigation.
* **Functional:** Every element must serve a purpose.
